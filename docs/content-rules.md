## Never publish on a demo site

<!-- TODO(owner): confirm and expand with your actual policy -->

- Real customer data of any kind, including anonymised extracts
- Screenshots containing real account numbers, names, balances, or internal IDs
- Unreleased product names, launch dates, or roadmap commitments
- Internal financial figures, headcount, or vendor contract terms
- Credentials, tokens, internal hostnames, or environment URLs
- Third-party logos or trademarks without clearance

If you are unsure whether something qualifies, it does. Ask before publishing.

## Always include

- A visible statement that this is an internal demo, not a product — the site
  footer does this by default, do not remove it
- A named owner with a working contact address
- A date or version, so a reader knows how current the content is

## Screenshots

Use synthetic data. Every screenshot on a demo site should be reproducible by
someone with no access to production.
